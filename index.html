SQL that turns evidence into decisions.

The Fraud Lab uses Python and SQLite to generate and analyze 3,771 synthetic transactions across 60 accounts. The Adverse Media Review Desk runs on SQLite / Cloudflare D1 with nine training cases and 27 evidence comparisons.

  • JOINs and foreign keys connect evidence to the right case.
  • CTEs and CASE make the triage rules inspectable.
  • GROUP BY and COUNT summarize matches, conflicts and missing identifiers.
  • JSON extraction and migrations read source attributes and maintain the schema.
