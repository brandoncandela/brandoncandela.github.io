# Portfolio design system

This site is a recruiter-facing financial crime portfolio. It should feel like an experienced investigator's casebook: precise, restrained, evidence-led and easy to review.

## Direction

- Dark navy surfaces reflect the investigation and fintech context without imitating a trading dashboard.
- Warm evidence gold identifies supporting proof, metadata and review context.
- Teal is reserved for actions, links and current state.
- Source Serif 4 gives major statements an editorial, human voice. IBM Plex Sans carries all working content. IBM Plex Mono is limited to code and data labels.
- The page uses rules, lists and editorial layouts more often than cards. Rounded containers are reserved for interactive controls, screenshots and the final contact panel.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#071119`
- Raised surface: `#12232f`
- Primary text: `#f2f5f4`
- Supporting text: `#b4c2c8`
- Action teal: `#65d9ba`
- Evidence gold: `#d7b77c`
- Border: `#263944`
- Corners: 6px for controls and media, 12px maximum for grouped surfaces
- Spacing follows an 8px base scale: 8, 12, 18, 24, 32, 48, 72 and 104px

## Content hierarchy

1. State Brandon's investigation point of view and role fit.
2. Show working projects and the decisions they support.
3. Establish professional experience and technical depth.
4. Show written reasoning, credentials and community leadership.
5. End with a direct contact path.

## Guardrails

- Preserve factual claims and clearly label synthetic or educational data.
- Avoid decorative gradients, glass effects, icon-card grids and equal visual weight for every project.
- Do not use monospace type as decoration.
- Keep all interactive targets at least 44px high and preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Maintain a useful reduced-motion experience rather than globally collapsing every transition.
